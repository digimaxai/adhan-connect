## 2. Your privacy, in everyday use

Adhan Connect helps you stay connected with your mosques. We respect the trust you place in us and aim to use only the information needed for the features you choose. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

**You do not need to sign up or provide a name or email address** to view prayer times, listen to live Adhan, follow mosques or plan attendance. Named sign-in is reserved for mosque staff, including local administrators and muezzins, and authorised platform administrators.

### Remembering your mosques

The app saves your mosque choices, prayer settings, event interactions and attendance plans, including party size. To remember these between visits, our authentication provider gives your app a persistent session identifier without requiring your name, email or password.

That identifier links your choices and any notification token to the same session. These records are still personal information, even without a name attached.

### Finding mosques nearby

If you allow location access, the app uses your coordinates to find nearby mosques, calculate prayer times or check nearby access to a live broadcast. Relevant requests can send coordinates to our servers or prayer-time providers.

If you enable travel alerts, we also save a rounded location, radius and expiry to match alerts to your chosen area. You can change device location permissions in your phone's settings.

### Receiving notifications

If you enable push alerts, we use a notification token to send messages to your device. Notification providers process the token, message content and delivery information. We also keep relevant notification preferences and delivery records. You can turn device notification permissions off in your phone's settings.

### Helping us keep things working

The app and its infrastructure process technical information such as app/device details, IP addresses, request logs and errors to operate securely and investigate problems.

If you contact us for help, report content or ask us to review a decision, we use the information you send, relevant evidence and a record of our response.

### If you manage or broadcast for a mosque

Staff accounts also involve a name, email, optional profile details, sign-in information and consent records. Our authentication provider manages sign-in and password hashing.

We process mosque roles, rota assignments, cover requests, broadcast activity, uploaded content and administrative records. Broadcasting transmits microphone audio through the broadcast service.

Information comes from you, your device, participating mosque administrators and our service providers. Public mosque details may also come from mosque publications and timetable services.

## 3. Why we use your information

Our purposes and legal bases are:

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Provide requested account, prayer, listening, preference and mosque-management services | Performance of our agreement with you, where the processing is necessary to provide that service |
| Optional location-based features and optional push alerts | Your consent |
| Protect the platform, investigate faults, prevent abuse, assess content reports and handle support or complaints | Our legitimate interests in running a safe, reliable service, balanced against your rights |
| Keep records required by law and respond to legally binding requests | Compliance with a legal obligation |
| Establish, exercise or defend legal claims, where necessary | Our legitimate interests; an additional legal condition where sensitive information is involved |

Following a mosque saves your choice to see its prayer times, events, campaigns and updates, and receive any alerts you enable. A follow does not establish your faith, membership or attendance. We use these choices to deliver the updates you request, not to assign you a religious identity.

Some other information, such as a plan to attend a particular prayer or a mosque staff role, may reveal religious beliefs or practice. Where these features involve that sensitive information, we rely on your **explicit consent**, in addition to the applicable legal basis above. If we need to retain sensitive information to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim, we rely on the applicable legal-claims condition for that limited purpose. Accepting this notice or giving a device permission is not, by itself, that explicit consent.

You can withdraw consent through the available privacy controls or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Features that need the relevant information may then become unavailable; withdrawal does not automatically delete all records.

## 4. Who helps us run the app

We use service providers for:

- **Cloud hosting, authentication and data storage:** to operate the app and store the account, session, preference and content records described above.
- **Push notification delivery:** to process notification tokens, message content and delivery information.
- **Live audio delivery:** to transmit broadcasts and process connection information, including through a mosque's chosen broadcast provider.
- **Prayer-time services:** to supply schedules using relevant mosque or device coordinates where needed.
- **Email and support:** to deliver service messages and handle correspondence and technical support.

We use contractual safeguards appropriate to each provider's role. You can contact us for more information about the providers that receive your information.

Authorised mosque staff receive information needed to manage their mosque and staff activities. Attendance summaries are intended to show totals rather than lists identifying individual attendees. Content published on mosque profiles is visible to app users. Please avoid including other people's personal information in public content without an appropriate basis.

When investigating a content report or complaint, we may share relevant details with the affected mosque, person or provider where necessary. We limit identifying details to what is needed, but cannot promise absolute confidentiality where disclosure is legally required.

A participating mosque may have its own responsibility for information it uses for mosque administration. Contact us if you need help identifying the relevant organisation.

We may disclose information where necessary to meet a legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can change notification and location permissions in device settings and use the app's available preference controls. Uninstalling the app does **not** automatically delete information on our servers.

You can contact **privacy@adhanconnect.app** to request access, correction or deletion, or to ask about restricting processing, objecting to processing or receiving a portable copy. These rights depend on the circumstances. You can also withdraw consent where we rely on it. We may need proportionate information to verify that a request relates to you. For use without a staff account, verification relies on control of that session rather than looking you up by name or email. If the session credential has been lost, we may be unable to identify which records belong to you.

**Your right to object:** You can object to processing based on our legitimate interests for reasons relating to your circumstances. Contact us using the details above; we will assess the objection and explain whether we must stop or have lawful grounds to continue.

Some records may need to be retained for a lawful reason. If we cannot fulfil all or part of a request, we will explain why. Operational handover for a mosque role does not remove your legal rights.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/), or another competent data protection authority where applicable.

## 7. Younger users and changes to this notice

Named staff accounts are intended for people aged 16 or over. This age rule does not, by itself, establish the age of people using the app without a staff account. If you believe we hold information about a younger child, please contact us.

We will update this notice when our practices change and make the current version available in the app or on our website. We will use an appropriate in-app or other notice to explain significant changes.
